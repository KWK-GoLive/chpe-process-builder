Sandbox · free build

Design your own plant

  1. Choose a process and read its spec card: conditions, separation and product.
  2. The reactor is already in place. Place raw-material tanks, process units and product tanks in their zones.
  3. Connect every outlet and set temperatures and pressures. Each unit's card shows what leaves it.
  4. Press Deploy. The flow stops where your plant does not work. Find out why and fix it.
